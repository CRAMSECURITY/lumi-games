# 014.3 PAYMENT SECURITY
Refund updates validate original owner, charge, payload, currency and amount, under BEGIN IMMEDIATE. Duplicate refunds are idempotent; unknown refunds direct users to payment support. Refunds do not recreate a missing subscription. Payment acknowledgement distinguishes duplicate receipts from unmatched receipts even when Premium is already active. Checkout checks profile, suspension and order in one query and rejects future timestamps. Empty payment identifiers are rejected. No schema or price change. Offline tests only; real Stars settlement, late payment after account deletion, out-of-order events, and reconciliation require staging validation. No automatic money movement or refunds introduced.
